# PLEASE HOLD - Game Design Document

## Overview
An incremental/idle game about being on hold, losing your mind, and accidentally ending the universe. Built as a vanilla JS browser game (no frameworks, no build tools).

**Design principle:** Separate concerns. Each system (dust, phase1, UI) is its own module. To tune balance, only edit the relevant file without touching the core game loop.

## Game Structure (3 Phases)

### Phase 1: The Call (~90-120 min active play)
- **Theme:** You're on hold disputing a $1.47 charge
- **Core verb:** Clicking (early), managing (mid), optimizing (late)
- **Resources:** Patience (primary), Will to Live (gate), Dust (secondary/slow)
- **Display timer:** Shows in-game time (minutes → hours → days → weeks → months → years)
- **Real timer:** Internal only, used for metrics

### Phase 2: The Escalation (~90-120 min) - NOT YET BUILT
- **Theme:** Extended warranty pitch. You're furious.
- **New resource:** Rage (replaces clicking as primary action)
- **WtL becomes:** Composure
- **Deep Breath becomes:** "Compose Yourself" or similar
- **Dust scale:** meters → kilometers → global

### Phase 3: The Geological (~60-120 min) - NOT YET BUILT
- **Theme:** You're beyond clicking. Resource allocation strategy.
- **New resource:** Inertia (generated by NOT acting)
- **Threat resource:** Entropy (must be managed or you "disconnect")
- **Dust scale:** planetary → cosmic

## Phase 1 Mechanics (Current Implementation)

### Resources
- **Patience:** Primary currency. Earned by clicking (+patiencePerClick) and passively (generators). Spent on: generators, upgrades, queue advances, Deep Breath.
- **Will to Live (WtL):** Gate resource. Each click costs 1 WtL (reduced by upgrades). Passive drain starts at 5 real minutes and escalates: `0.15 * log2(realMinutes - 4)`. Regen from upgrades. At 0 → hangup.
- **Dust (particles):** Secondary resource. Accumulates based on in-game time with a SEPARATE cap (x10 time mult max for dust specifically). Spent on Dust Collectors.

### Generators ("Coping Mechanisms")
Repeatable purchases. Cost formula: `baseCost × growthRate^owned`
Soft cap at threshold: post-cap growth = `growthRate^8` (extremely punishing)

| Name | Base Cost | Growth | Production | Soft Cap | Unlock At |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|----------|-----------|
| Doodle Pad | 15 | 1.18 | 0.1/s | 15 | 0 |
| Fidget Spinner | 100 | 1.17 | 0.35/s | 15 | 50 maxP |
| Autodialer | 600 | 1.16 | 2.0/s | 18 | 400 maxP |
| Speed Dialer | 5000 | 1.15 | 10.0/s | 18 | 4000 maxP |
| Robo-Caller | 40000 | 1.14 | 50.0/s | 15 | 30000 maxP |
| Shadow Call Center | 350000 | 1.13 | 300.0/s | 12 | 250000 maxP |

### Upgrades (One-time purchases, patience-based)
15 upgrades ranging from 50 to 4,000,000 patience. Include:
- Snack Drawer (cheaper Deep Breath)
- Hold Music Tolerance (+click power)
- Colored Pencils (Doodle Pads x2)
- Comfortable Chair (+WtL max, +regen)
- Rhythmic Clicking (unlocks combo system)
- Titanium Bearings (Fidget Spinners x2)
- Caffeine IV Drip (+click, halve WtL cost)
- Parallel Lines (Autodialers x2)
- Second Phone Line (ALL generators x2)
- Overclocked Modem (Speed Dialers x3)
- Entropy Noticed (starts dust accumulation)
- Minutes Feel Like Hours (time x10)
- Machine Learning (Robo-Callers x3)
- Time Perception Decay (time x10)
- Conference Call (ALL generators x2)
- Days Blur Into Weeks (time x12)
- Corporate Insider (clicking costs 0 WtL)

### Dust Collectors (Dust-purchased, in dust.js)
11 items, costs 200 to 150,000 particles. Mix of:
- Patience% boosts (globalGenMultiplier)
- Dust/sec increases
- WtL regen/max increases
- Queue cost reducers

### Queue
- Start: #150
- Cost: `30 × 1.095^advances × queueCostMult`
- At queue 0 → Phase 1 complete → transition screen

### Time System
- `effectiveTimeMult = state.timeMultiplier × dustTimeFactor`
- Time upgrades: x10 ("Minutes Feel Like Hours") × x10 ("Time Perception Decay") × x12 ("Days Blur Into Weeks") = x1200 base
- `dustTimeFactor = min(50000, 1 + log10(maxDust+1)² × 10)` — uses MAX dust (never drops)
- Dust accumulation uses SEPARATE cap: `min(10, effectiveTimeMult)` — prevents dust explosion
- Phone tiers trigger at in-game time thresholds (1 week, 3 months, 5 years)
- Target: Phase 1 ends at ~10 years in-game time

### WtL Passive Drain
- Starts at 5 real minutes
- Rate: `0.15 × log2(realMinutes - 4)` per second
- At 6 min: ~0.15/s. At 15 min: ~0.5/s. At 30 min: ~0.7/s. At 60 min: ~0.87/s.
- Regen from: Comfortable Chair (+0.3), Dust Collectors (various)
- Corporate Insider removes CLICK cost but NOT passive drain

### Hangup Penalty
- Triggers at wtl < 0.1
- Patience → 0
- Queue falls back by penalty spots
- BUT queueAdvances stays same (cost to re-advance is high)
- WtL refills to max

### Click Combo (unlocked by Rhythmic Clicking upgrade)
- Max: x4
- Build: +0.3 per click
- Decay: 0.4/sec when not clicking
- Multiplies patience/sec

### Phone Tiers (cosmetic + small bonus, by in-game time)
- Tin Can & String (start)
- Rotary Phone (1 week in-game)
- Landline (3 months in-game)
- Cordless Phone (5 years in-game) — likely Phase 2 territory

- Dust collectors column header: "Dust Collectors" ✓

### Balance (Sim-Verified)
- Phase 1 completion: ~98 min active player (target 90-120 ✓)
- In-game time at end: ~10 years (target ✓)
- Upgrades: 17 total, spread every 3-7 min ✓
- Dust collectors: 11 total, costs 200-150,000, spread over 30 min ✓
- Queue momentum prevents dead zone at end ✓
- WtL drain escalates after 30 min with linear component ✓
- Soft cap: growthRate^8 post-cap ✓

### Remaining Issues
- WtL regen from collectors still exceeds drain after Corporate Insider in very late game
- Clicking at +4/click becomes irrelevant at 100K+ pps (design intent: automation takes over)
- After Corporate Insider, Deep Breath needs repurposing or the passive drain needs to threaten more
- Need indicator showing WHAT is draining WtL (not just that it's happening)
- Dust starts too fast after Entropy Noticed (first collector available almost immediately)

### Design Decisions Made
- Dust accumulates in in-game time (narratively correct) but with x10 cap specifically for dust
- Time display uses maxDust for factor (spending dust never drops display time backward)
- "Coping Mechanisms" is the column name for generators (not "Generators")
- "Dust Collectors" is the name for the dust shop (you're collecting/cleaning dust)
- Deep Breath stays named "Deep Breath" in Phase 1. Phase 2 will rename to something anger-related.
- Phase 1 ends showing ~10 years of in-game time
- Time upgrades: x10 × x10 × x12 = x1200 base (with dustTimeFactor on top)
- Doodle Pad production: 0.1 (clean mental math)
- Queue Momentum: +2% discount per advance, caps at 30%, decays after 10s idle

### Simulator

## Phase 2 Design (Planned, Not Built)
- Extended warranty call triggers rage
- Click button → [ DEMAND ]
- Deep Breath → "Compose Yourself" (generates Rage)
- WtL → Composure
- Rage spent on: escalation upgrades, queue shortcuts, dust accelerators
- Multiple queue resets (transferred between departments)
- Dust goes from particles → covers your room → neighborhood → global
- Ends with being transferred to a department that "doesn't have a name"

## Phase 3 Design (Planned, Not Built)
- No clicking. Pure strategy/allocation.
- Resources: Dust (cosmic scale), Patience (massive passive), Inertia (builds when idle), Entropy (threat)
- Allocation sliders: Dust Generators, Entropy Dampeners, Patience Converters, etc.
- Tradeoffs: more dust = more entropy. Must balance.
- Win condition: some cosmic resolution of the $1.47

## The Ending (Phase 3 End)
The hold music stops. Someone answers. "Your refund has been processed." The timestamp is from before the universe began. Time on hold: 13.8 billion years. "It was the principle of the thing."

## Tone
Dry, absurd humor. Played completely straight. The mundane horror of bureaucracy scaled to cosmic proportions.
